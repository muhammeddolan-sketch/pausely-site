Pausely Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026 · pausely home

Pausely does not collect your data. Period.

There is no account, no cloud, no analytics SDK and no advertising in Pausely. Everything you log — symptoms, cycle events, lifestyle tags, settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, sell it, or hand it to anyone, because we never have it.

What is stored, and where

DataWhere it livesWho can access it
Symptom, cycle and trigger logsYour device only (local database)Only you
App settings (reminder time, tracked symptoms)Your device onlyOnly you
Backups you exportWherever you choose to save themOnly you (encrypted with your passphrase)

Backups

When you export a backup, Pausely creates a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose (AES-256-GCM). The file is useless without the passphrase. Where you store that file — and who you share it with — is entirely your choice. We never receive a copy.

PDF reports

Doctor reports are generated on your device. Sharing them is initiated by you through your phone's standard share menu. Pausely never transmits them anywhere on its own.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Pausely Premium, the purchase is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive anonymous transaction records from the store (required to unlock your purchase) but never your payment details or identity.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Pausely does not use push notification services.

Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device, exercising your rights is simple: deleting the app deletes all data. Exporting a backup gives you a full copy of everything.

Medical disclaimer

Pausely is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Patterns shown in the app are statistical observations of your own logs, not clinical conclusions. Always consult a healthcare professional about your symptoms.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be shown in the app before taking effect. Since Pausely's architecture has no data collection, meaningful changes are unlikely.
